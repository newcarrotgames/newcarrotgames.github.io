How socketing works

Gems go into sockets on weapons, tools and armor. Each gem has a tier, and each socket has a tier, and the gem only works in the gear slots shown on its card.

1Find gear with sockets

  • About 40% of gear from chests and mob drops tries to roll sockets. Materials with high enchantability (gold, diamond) roll more.
  • Loot rolls up to 5 sockets on armor, swords and shields, 3 on axes, 1 on bows, pickaxes and shovels.
  • Infernal mobs, Doomlike, Roguelike and Recurrent Complex structures can all give socketed gear.
  • High-level Lycanites bosses drop 3 to 9 loose sockets: Amalgalich at level 500+, Asmodeus at 1000+, Rahovart at 2000+.
  • To add a loose socket to gear, put both in a crafting grid. No item can go over 5.

2Match the socket tier

A socket takes gems of its own tier or lower. Loot sockets roll Common 30%, Uncommon 50%, Rare 20%.

Gem combos

Put every gem of a combo into sockets on the same item, in any order. The combo replaces those gems' own effects with the effect below.

Numbers come straight from the server's Socketed config files (Dregora Parasited, Oct 21 2026 #2 release). Card titles are the names the game shows for each socketed gem. Durations are in seconds, and "constant" means the effect keeps refreshing while you wear the gear.
